## Course Description 
An introduction to theory of prices and the allocation of resources. Topics include the pricing of goods and services, the determination of wages and returns to capital, market structure, and international trade. Credit Hours: 3

This course is required by economics majors and all business majors. Class consisted of 38 primarily first-year students.

## Course Goals
1. Understand Economics jargon in everyday discours
2. Evaluate decision-making using economic logic

## Course Learning Objectives
After completing this course, students will be able to
1. Understand and explain key terms in Economics and its underlying logic
2. Illustrate demand and supply curves given underlying consumer and firm behavior
3. Calculate equilibria of demand and supply via price and quantity traded in a market given various market shifts
4. Perform basic welfare analysis of a market given its conditions, demand curve, and supply curve
5. Determine optimal decision-making points for consumers and/or firms given all constraints
6. Compare and contrast firm and consumer outcomes under multiple industry structures of competition
