## About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in economics at Tulane University expecting degree conferral in May 2027. My research fields are Health Economics, Behavioral Economics, and Applied Microeconomics. My research studies individuals’ health behaviors to speak toward broad economic questions around uncertainty and intertemporal choice in individual decision-making. I am on the 2026-27 job market, and my job market paper analyzes how individuals’ trust in the medical system shifts their health behaviors.
